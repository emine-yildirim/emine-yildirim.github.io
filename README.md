Hi! I am an Asst. Prof. in [Galatasaray Üniversitesi](https://matematik.gsu.edu.tr/tr/hakkimizda/bolum-hakkinda#). 

I completed my PhD at the [LACIM](http://lacim.uqam.ca/) with [Hugh Thomas](https://uqam.academia.edu/HughThomas) in 2018. 


A list of my previous experiences:

- a researcher in  [the Quantum Groups and Cluster Algebras group](https://icms.bg/research-groups/quantum-groups-and-cluster-algebras/) at [the International Center for Mathematical Sciences (ICMS) - Sofia](https://icms.bg/).
- a research fellow in the [School of Mathematics](https://eps.leeds.ac.uk/maths) at the University of Leeds.
- an [INI](https://www.newton.ac.uk)-Simons Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the [DPMMS](https://www.dpmms.cam.ac.uk/), University of Cambridge. 
- a Coleman Research Fellow at the [Department of Mathematics and Statistics](https://www.queensu.ca/mathstat/), Queen's University.
